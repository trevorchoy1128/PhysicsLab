磁通量與磁通匝鏈數 Magnetic Flux and Flux Linkage

$$ \Phi = BA \cos \theta $$
磁通匝鏈數Magnetic flux linkage $$ = N\Phi = NBA \cos \theta $$
變數與單位Symbols & Units
  • $\Phi$磁通量（單位：韋伯 $\text{Wb}$）Magnetic flux (Unit: weber $\text{Wb}$)
  • $B$磁場強度或磁通量密度（單位：$\text{T}$ 或 $\text{Wb m}^{-2}$）Magnetic field strength or magnetic flux density (Unit: $\text{T}$ or $\text{Wb m}^{-2}$)
  • $A$面積（單位：$\text{m}^2$）Area (Unit: $\text{m}^2$)
  • $\theta$磁場 $B$ 與線圈法線的夾角（單位：$^\circ$）Angle between magnetic field $B$ and the normal of the coil (Unit: $^\circ$)
  • $N$線圈的匝數Number of turns of the coil
📝 應用範例 📝 Example

面積為 $4 \text{ cm}^2$ 的 $10$ 匝線圈處於磁通量密度為 $2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ T}$ 的勻強磁場中。求線圈的法線與磁場互相平行時通過線圈的磁通匝鏈數。 A $10$-turn coil of area $4 \text{ cm}^2$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field of flux density $2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ T}$. Find the flux linkage through the coil if the normal of the coil is parallel to the field.

【詳解】[Solution]
磁通匝鏈數Flux linkage $$ = N\Phi = N(BA) = (10)(2 \times 10^{-3})(4 \times 10^{-4}) = 8 \times 10^{-6} \text{ Wb} $$
💪 隨堂練習 💪 Practice

面積為 $10 \text{ cm}^2$ 的單匝圓形線圈置於磁通量密度為 $0.02 \text{ Wb m}^{-2}$ 的勻強磁場中，磁場垂直通過線圈。通過線圈的磁通量是多少？ A single-turn circular coil of area $10 \text{ cm}^2$ is put inside a uniform magnetic field of magnetic flux density $0.02 \text{ Wb m}^{-2}$. The magnetic field passes through the coil perpendicularly. What is the magnetic flux through the coil?

【答案】[Answer]
$$ \Phi = BA \cos \theta = (0.02)(10 \times 10^{-4}) \cos 0^\circ = 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ Wb} $$
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz

一個面積為 $A$ 的導電環在勻強磁場中轉動 $90^\circ$，變成與磁場平行。整個過程歷時 $0.2 \text{ s}$，導電環內感生出的平均電動勢為 $\varepsilon$。估算在轉動之前，通過環的磁通量。 A conducting ring of area $A$ rotates $90^\circ$ in a uniform magnetic field so that the ring becomes parallel to the field. The process takes $0.2 \text{ s}$ and an average e.m.f. of $\varepsilon$ is induced in the ring. Estimate the magnetic flux through the ring before the rotation.

$$ 5\varepsilon A $$
$$ \frac{\varepsilon}{5A} $$
$$ \frac{\varepsilon}{5} $$
$$ 5\varepsilon $$
【解析】初始磁通量為 $\Phi$，轉動後磁場與環平行，磁通量變為 $0$。根據 $\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$，可得 $\Delta\Phi = \varepsilon \times 0.2 = \frac{\varepsilon}{5}$。因此轉動前的磁通量 $\Phi = \frac{\varepsilon}{5}$。 [Explanation] The initial flux is $\Phi$, and becomes $0$ after rotation when the ring is parallel to the field. According to $\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$, we have $\Delta\Phi = \varepsilon \times 0.2 = \frac{\varepsilon}{5}$. Therefore, the magnetic flux before rotation is $\Phi = \frac{\varepsilon}{5}$.

法拉第電磁感應定律 Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction

$$ \varepsilon = \frac{\Delta \Phi}{\Delta t} $$
對於 $N$ 匝的線圈For a coil of $N$ turns $$ \varepsilon = N \frac{\Delta \Phi}{\Delta t} = \frac{\Delta (N\Phi)}{\Delta t} $$
變數與單位Symbols & Units
  • $\varepsilon$感生電動勢（單位：$\text{V}$）Induced e.m.f. (Unit: $\text{V}$)
  • $\Delta \Phi$磁通量的變化（單位：$\text{Wb}$）Change in magnetic flux (Unit: $\text{Wb}$)
  • $\Delta t$時間的變化（單位：$\text{s}$）Change in time (Unit: $\text{s}$)
  • $N$線圈的匝數Number of turns of the coil
📝 應用範例 📝 Example

面積為 $0.01 \text{ m}^2$ 的 $10$ 匝方形線圈置於 $4 \text{ mT}$ 的勻強磁場內，磁場指入頁面。求磁場在 $0.2 \text{ s}$ 內降至零時，線圈中感生電動勢的平均值。 A $10$-turn square coil of area $0.01 \text{ m}^2$ is inside a uniform magnetic field of $4 \text{ mT}$ pointing into the page. Find the average value of the induced e.m.f. in the coil if the magnetic field decreases to zero in $0.2 \text{ s}$.

【詳解】[Solution]
$$ \varepsilon = \frac{\Delta(N\Phi)}{\Delta t} = NA \frac{\Delta B}{\Delta t} = (10)(0.01)\left(\frac{4 \times 10^{-3} - 0}{0.2}\right) = 2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ V} $$
💪 隨堂練習 💪 Practice

面積為 $20 \text{ cm}^2$ 的單匝線圈置於磁場 $B$ 內，磁場與線圈的法線互相平行。在 $0.1 \text{ s}$ 內，磁場的變化 $\Delta B$ 是 $10 \text{ mT}$。求感生電動勢的量值 $\varepsilon$。 A single-turn coil of area $20 \text{ cm}^2$ is placed in a magnetic field $B$ parallel to its normal. The change in magnetic field $\Delta B$ in $0.1 \text{ s}$ is $10 \text{ mT}$. Find the magnitude of the induced e.m.f. $\varepsilon$.

【答案】[Answer]
$$ \varepsilon = NA \frac{\Delta B}{\Delta t} = (1)(20 \times 10^{-4})\left(\frac{10 \times 10^{-3}}{0.1}\right) = 2 \times 10^{-4} \text{ V} = 0.2 \text{ mV} $$
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz

一個矩形金屬框的面積是 $A$，置於勻強磁場 $B$ 之中。金屬框的平面與磁場之間的夾角是 $60^\circ$。時間 $t$ 內，磁場的磁通量密度穩定地從 $B$ 減至零。求該時段內金屬框中所產生的感生電動勢。 A rectangular metal frame of area $A$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field $B$. The angle between the field and the plane of the frame is $60^\circ$. The flux density of the field decreases at a constant rate from $B$ to zero in time $t$. Find the e.m.f. induced in the frame in this period of time.

$$ \frac{BA}{2t} $$
$$ \frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2t} $$
$$ \frac{BA}{\sqrt{3}t} $$
$$ \frac{2BA}{\sqrt{3}t} $$
【解析】金屬框平面與磁場夾角為 $60^\circ$，因此法線與磁場夾角為 $30^\circ$。初始磁通量 $\Phi = BA \cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2}$。感生電動勢 $\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2} - 0}{t} = \frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2t}$。 [Explanation] The angle between the plane and the field is $60^\circ$, so the angle between the normal and the field is $30^\circ$. Initial flux $\Phi = BA \cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2}$. Induced e.m.f. $\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2} - 0}{t} = \frac{\sqrt{3}BA}{2t}$.

導體切割磁力線 Conductor Cutting Magnetic Field Lines

$$ \varepsilon = BLv $$
變數與單位Symbols & Units
  • $\varepsilon$感生電動勢（單位：$\text{V}$）Induced e.m.f. (Unit: $\text{V}$)
  • $B$磁場強度（單位：$\text{T}$）Magnetic field strength (Unit: $\text{T}$)
  • $L$磁場中導體的長度（單位：$\text{m}$）Length of the conductor in the magnetic field (Unit: $\text{m}$)
  • $v$導體垂直切割磁力線的速率（單位：$\text{m s}^{-1}$）Speed of the conductor cutting through the magnetic field lines perpendicularly (Unit: $\text{m s}^{-1}$)
📝 應用範例 📝 Example

勻強磁場內，金屬棒以恆速率 $5 \text{ cm s}^{-1}$ 沿兩條光滑的平行金屬導軌向右移動。磁場指入頁面，磁通量密度為 $1.5 \text{ mT}$。金屬棒長 $16 \text{ cm}$，兩條導軌相距 $12 \text{ cm}$。求兩條導軌之間的金屬棒兩端的感生電動勢。 A metal rod moves to the right along two smooth parallel metal rails at a constant speed of $5 \text{ cm s}^{-1}$ in a uniform magnetic field. The field is pointing into the page and of flux density $1.5 \text{ mT}$. The rod is $16 \text{ cm}$ long and the two rails are $12 \text{ cm}$ apart. Find the induced e.m.f. across the part of the rod between the rails.

【詳解】[Solution]
$$ \varepsilon = BLv = (1.5 \times 10^{-3})(0.12)(0.05) = 9 \times 10^{-6} \text{ V} $$
💪 隨堂練習 💪 Practice

在 $5 \text{ mT}$ 的勻強磁場中，一根長 $10 \text{ cm}$ 的導電棒在 $0.5 \text{ s}$ 內向右移動 $8 \text{ cm}$。導電棒與磁場互相垂直。導電棒兩端的感生電動勢的量值是多少？ A conducting rod $10 \text{ cm}$ long moves $8 \text{ cm}$ to the right in $0.5 \text{ s}$ in a uniform magnetic field of $5 \text{ mT}$. The rod is perpendicular to the field. What is the magnitude of the induced e.m.f. across the rod?

【答案】[Answer]
$$ v = \frac{0.08}{0.5} = 0.16 \text{ m s}^{-1} $$ $$ \varepsilon = BLv = (5 \times 10^{-3})(0.10)(0.16) = 8 \times 10^{-5} \text{ V} $$
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz

長度為 $L$ 的金屬棒 $XY$ 以恆速度 $v$ 在勻強磁場 $B$ 中移動，磁場指入頁面，金屬棒向右移動。金屬棒與磁場垂直，但與 $v$成 $30^\circ$ 角。求 $XY$ 之間的電勢差。 A metal rod $XY$ of length $L$ is moving in a uniform magnetic field $B$ at a constant velocity $v$. The magnetic field points into the page and the rod moves to the right. The rod is perpendicular to the magnetic field and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ to $v$. Find the potential difference across $XY$.

$$ 0 $$
$$ BLv \sin 30^\circ $$
$$ BLv \cos 30^\circ $$
$$ BLv $$
【解析】金屬棒垂直切割磁力線的有效速率分量為 $v \sin 30^\circ$。根據 $\varepsilon = BLv_{\text{perpendicular}}$，電勢差為 $BLv \sin 30^\circ$。 [Explanation] The effective velocity component perpendicular to the rod is $v \sin 30^\circ$. According to $\varepsilon = BLv_{\text{perpendicular}}$, the potential difference is $BLv \sin 30^\circ$.
