功率與市電電壓Power and Mains Voltage

$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$
$$R = \frac{V^2}{P}$$
變數與單位Symbol List
  • $P$功率 (單位：$\text{W}$)Power (Unit: $\text{W}$)
  • $V$電壓 (單位：$\text{V}$)Voltage (Unit: $\text{V}$)
  • $R$電阻 (單位：$\Omega$)Resistance (Unit: $\Omega$)
📌 應用範例📌 Example
電熨斗的額定值是「$220\ \text{V}, 1800\ \text{W}$」。
(a) 求電熨斗的電阻。
(b) 電熨斗在美國使用，美國的市電電壓為 $120\ \text{V}$。求電熨斗消耗的功率。 An electric iron is rated at '$220\ \text{V}, 1800\ \text{W}$'.
(a) Find the resistance of the iron.
(b) The iron is used in the USA where the mains voltage is $120\ \text{V}$. Find the power consumed by the iron.
(a) $R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{220^2}{1800} = 26.9\ \Omega$
(b) $P = \frac{V^2}{R} = \frac{120^2}{26.9} = 535\ \text{W}$ (a) $R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{220^2}{1800} = 26.9\ \Omega$
(b) $P = \frac{V^2}{R} = \frac{120^2}{26.9} = 535\ \text{W}$
✍️ 隨堂練習✍️ Practice
電水煲的額定值是「$220\ \text{V}, 1500\ \text{W}$」，連接到 $110\ \text{V}$ 的市電時，需要 $6$ 分鐘才能煮沸一定分量的水。
(a) 求電水煲的電阻。 An electric kettle rated '$220\ \text{V}, 1500\ \text{W}$' takes $6$ minutes to boil a certain amount of water when connected to a $110\ \text{V}$ mains supply.
(a) Find its resistance.
(a) $R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{220^2}{1500} = 32.3\ \Omega$ (a) $R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{220^2}{1500} = 32.3\ \Omega$

電能消耗Energy Consumption

$$E = Pt$$
變數與單位Symbol List
  • $E$電能 (單位：$\text{J}$ 或 $\text{kW h}$)Electrical energy (Unit: $\text{J}$ or $\text{kW h}$)
  • $P$功率 (單位：$\text{W}$ 或 $\text{kW}$)Power (Unit: $\text{W}$ or $\text{kW}$)
  • $t$時間 (單位：$\text{s}$ 或 $\text{h}$)Time (Unit: $\text{s}$ or $\text{h}$)
📌 應用範例📌 Example
電暖爐消耗 $2\ \text{kW}$ 的功率，並開着了 $45$ 分鐘。
求消耗的電能，答案以 $\text{kW h}$ 為單位。 An electric heater consumes a power of $2\ \text{kW}$ and is switched on for $45$ minutes.
Find the electrical energy consumed in $\text{kW h}$.
$E = Pt = (2\ \text{kW}) \times \left(\frac{45}{60}\ \text{h}\right) = 1.5\ \text{kW h}$ $E = Pt = (2\ \text{kW}) \times \left(\frac{45}{60}\ \text{h}\right) = 1.5\ \text{kW h}$
✍️ 隨堂練習✍️ Practice
電視機消耗的功率為 $100\ \text{W}$。求電視機開着 $500$ 小時消耗的電能（以 $\text{kW h}$ 為單位）。 The power consumed by a TV is $100\ \text{W}$. Find the electrical energy consumed (in $\text{kW h}$) for operating the TV for $500$ hours.
$E = Pt = (0.1\ \text{kW}) \times (500\ \text{h}) = 50\ \text{kW h}$ $E = Pt = (0.1\ \text{kW}) \times (500\ \text{h}) = 50\ \text{kW h}$
🎯 概念測驗🎯 Quiz
下列哪個單位與其他單位不同？ Which unit is different from the others?
A $\text{J}$
B $\text{kW h}$
C $\text{W}$
D $\text{W s}$
答對了！$\text{W}$ 是功率的單位，而 $\text{J}$, $\text{kW h}$ 和 $\text{W s}$ 都是能量的單位。 Correct! $\text{W}$ is a unit of power, while $\text{J}$, $\text{kW h}$ and $\text{W s}$ are units of energy.

交流電Alternating Current

$$V_{rms} = \frac{V_p}{\sqrt{2}}$$
$$I_{rms} = \frac{I_p}{\sqrt{2}} \quad , \quad \overline{P} = V_{rms} I_{rms} = \frac{V_{rms}^2}{R}$$
變數與單位Symbol List
  • $V_{rms}$方均根電壓 (單位：$\text{V}$)R.m.s. voltage (Unit: $\text{V}$)
  • $V_p$峯值電壓 (單位：$\text{V}$)Peak voltage (Unit: $\text{V}$)
  • $I_{rms}$方均根電流 (單位：$\text{A}$)R.m.s. current (Unit: $\text{A}$)
  • $I_p$峯值電流 (單位：$\text{A}$)Peak current (Unit: $\text{A}$)
  • $\overline{P}$平均功率 (單位：$\text{W}$)Average power (Unit: $\text{W}$)
📌 應用範例📌 Example
焗爐的額定值是「$220\ \text{V}, 1500\ \text{W}$」，連接到電壓方均根值為 $220\ \text{V}$，頻率為 $50\ \text{Hz}$ 的市電。
求流過焗爐的最大瞬時電流（即峯值電流）$I_p$。 An oven is rated at '$220\ \text{V}, 1500\ \text{W}$'. It is connected to the mains which has a r.m.s. voltage of $220\ \text{V}$ and a frequency of $50\ \text{Hz}$.
Find the maximum instantaneous current (i.e. peak current) $I_p$ through the oven.
$I_{rms} = \frac{\overline{P}}{V_{rms}} = \frac{1500}{220} = 6.82\ \text{A}$
$I_p = \sqrt{2} I_{rms} = \sqrt{2} \times 6.82 = 9.64\ \text{A}$ $I_{rms} = \frac{\overline{P}}{V_{rms}} = \frac{1500}{220} = 6.82\ \text{A}$
$I_p = \sqrt{2} I_{rms} = \sqrt{2} \times 6.82 = 9.64\ \text{A}$
✍️ 隨堂練習✍️ Practice
考慮一盞連接到正弦交流電源的電燈，峯值電流是 $I_p$。若 $I_p = 15\ \text{A}$，求 $I_{rms}$。 Consider a lamp connected to a sinusoidal a.c. power supply. The peak current is $I_p$. If $I_p = 15\ \text{A}$, find $I_{rms}$.
$I_{rms} = \frac{I_p}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{15}{\sqrt{2}} = 10.6\ \text{A}$ $I_{rms} = \frac{I_p}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{15}{\sqrt{2}} = 10.6\ \text{A}$
🎯 概念測驗🎯 Quiz
某燈泡的額定值是「$220\ \text{V}, 100\ \text{W}$」。如果燈泡接駁到峯值電壓為 $220\ \text{V}$ 的正弦交流電源，燈泡消耗的平均功率是多少？ A '$220\ \text{V}, 100\ \text{W}$' light bulb is connected to a power supply providing sinusoidal a.c. with a peak voltage equal to $220\ \text{V}$. What is the average power consumed by the bulb?
A $50\ \text{W}$
B $70.7\ \text{W}$
C $100\ \text{W}$
D $141\ \text{W}$
答對了！方均根電壓 $V_{rms} = \frac{220}{\sqrt{2}}\ \text{V}$，平均功率 $\overline{P} = \frac{V_{rms}^2}{R} = 50\ \text{W}$。 Correct! The r.m.s. voltage $V_{rms} = \frac{220}{\sqrt{2}}\ \text{V}$, so the average power $\overline{P} = \frac{V_{rms}^2}{R} = 50\ \text{W}$.
