作用於載電流導體的磁力 Magnetic force on current-carrying conductor

$$F = BIl \sin \theta$$
變形公式： 若導體與磁場垂直（$\theta = 90^\circ$），則 $F = BIl$。
Derived formula: If the conductor is perpendicular to the magnetic field ($\theta = 90^\circ$), then $F = BIl$.
定義： 載電流導體在磁場內會受磁力作用。
Definition: A current-carrying conductor experiences a magnetic force in a magnetic field.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $F$
    ：磁力，單位：牛頓 ($\text{N}$) : magnetic force, unit: newton ($\text{N}$)
  • $B$
    ：磁場，單位：忒斯拉 ($\text{T}$) : magnetic field, unit: tesla ($\text{T}$)
  • $I$
    ：電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $l$
    ：磁場內的導體長度，單位：米 ($\text{m}$) : length of conductor in B-field, unit: meter ($\text{m}$)
  • $\theta$
    ：$B$ 和 $I$ 之間的夾角，單位：度 ($^\circ$) : angle between $B$ and $I$, unit: degree ($^\circ$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：一支長 $0.15\ \text{m}$ 的金屬棒置於 $0.001\ \text{T}$ 的勻強磁場中。已知有 $0.2\ \text{A}$ 的電流通過金屬棒。求金屬棒與磁場形成 $30^\circ$ 夾角時，作用於金屬棒的磁力 $F$ 的量值。 Question: A conducting rod of length $0.15\ \text{m}$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field of $0.001\ \text{T}$. Given that a current of $0.2\ \text{A}$ passes through the rod, find the magnitude $F$ of the magnetic force acting on the rod when it makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the magnetic field.
題解：
Solution:
$$F = BIl \sin \theta = 0.001 \times 0.2 \times 0.15 \times \sin 30^\circ = 1.5 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{N}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：找出作用於導體的磁力的量值。導體在磁場中的長度是 $0.2\ \text{m}$。磁場 $B$ 為 $1.2\ \text{mT}$，電流 $I$ 為 $4\ \text{A}$，兩者互相垂直。 Question: Find the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the conductor. The length of the conductor within the magnetic field is $0.2\ \text{m}$. The magnetic field $B$ is $1.2\ \text{mT}$ and current $I$ is $4\ \text{A}$, which are perpendicular to each other.
題解：
Solution:
$$F = BIl \sin \theta = 1.2 \times 10^{-3} \times 4 \times 0.2 \times \sin 90^\circ = 9.6 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{N}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
當磁場 $B$ 和電流 $I$ 互相平行時（$\theta = 0^\circ$），作用於直導線的磁力 $F$ 是多少？ When the magnetic field $B$ and the current $I$ are parallel to each other ($\theta = 0^\circ$), what is the magnetic force $F$ acting on the straight wire?
最大值 Maximum
零 Zero
無法確定 Cannot be determined
取決於導線的長度 $l$ Depends on the length $l$
✅ 正確！當 $B$ 和 $I$ 互相平行（$\theta = 0^\circ$）時，$\sin \theta = 0$，$F$ 便等於零。 ✅ Correct! When $B$ and $I$ are parallel ($\theta = 0^\circ$), $\sin \theta = 0$ and $F$ is equal to zero.

線圈的轉動效應 Turning effect on a coil

$$\tau = BIAN \sin \phi$$
變形公式： 若線圈處於水平位置（$\phi = 90^\circ$），最大力矩 $\tau = BIAN$。
Derived formula: When the coil is horizontal ($\phi = 90^\circ$), the maximum moment $\tau = BIAN$.
定義： 磁場內，作用於載電流線圈兩邊的磁力對線圈產生轉動效應。
Definition: A current-carrying coil in a magnetic field experiences a turning effect due to the magnetic forces acting on its two sides.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $\tau$
    ：力矩，單位：牛頓·米 ($\text{N m}$) : moment, unit: newton-meter ($\text{N m}$)
  • $B$
    ：磁通量密度，單位：忒斯拉 ($\text{T}$) : magnetic flux density, unit: tesla ($\text{T}$)
  • $I$
    ：電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $A$
    ：線圈的面積，單位：平方米 ($\text{m}^2$) : area of the coil, unit: square meter ($\text{m}^2$)
  • $N$
    ：線圈的匝數 : number of turns of the coil
  • $\phi$
    ：線圈的法線與 $B$ 之間的夾角，單位：度 ($^\circ$) : angle between the normal of the coil and $B$, unit: degree ($^\circ$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：矩形線圈有 $20$ 匝，面積是 $30\ \text{cm}^2$，置於 $2\ \text{mT}$ 的勻強磁場 $B$ 內。通過線圈的電流是 $5\ \text{A}$。找出 $\theta = 30^\circ$ 時作用於線圈的力矩。 Question: A rectangular coil of $20$ turns and an area of $30\ \text{cm}^2$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field $B$ of $2\ \text{mT}$. The current flowing through the coil is $5\ \text{A}$. Find the moment acting on the coil when $\theta = 30^\circ$.
題解：
Solution:
$$\tau = BIAN \sin \phi = (2 \times 10^{-3}) \times 5 \times (30 \times 10^{-4}) \times 20 \times \sin 30^\circ = 3 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{N m}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：一個 $15$ 匝的線圈長 $16\ \text{cm}$，闊 $12\ \text{cm}$。電流 $I = 3\ \text{A}$，磁場 $B = 1\ \text{mT}$。線圈平面與磁場平行時，找出磁力沿轉軸作用於線圈的力矩。 Question: A $15$-turn coil of a size $16\ \text{cm}$ by $12\ \text{cm}$ carries current $I = 3\ \text{A}$ in a magnetic field $B = 1\ \text{mT}$. Find the moment acting on the coil about the axis of rotation when the plane of the coil is parallel to the magnetic field.
題解：
Solution:
$$A = 0.16 \times 0.12 = 0.0192\ \text{m}^2$$ $$\tau = BIAN \sin 90^\circ = (1 \times 10^{-3}) \times 3 \times 0.0192 \times 15 = 8.64 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{N m}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
下列哪項能增加直流電動機的轉動效應？ Which of the following can increase the turning effect of a d.c. motor?
減少電流 Decreasing the current
減少線圈的面積 Decreasing the area of the coil
增加線圈的匝數 Increasing the number of turns of the coil
使用較弱的磁鐵 Using a weaker magnet
✅ 正確！根據 $\tau = BIAN \sin \phi$，當 $B$、$I$、$A$ 或 $N$ 增加，電動機的轉動效應便會增加。 ✅ Correct! By $\tau = BIAN \sin \phi$, the turning effect of a motor increases when $B$, $I$, $A$ or $N$ increases.

兩條平行長直導線之間的磁力 Magnetic forces between two parallel wires

$$F_l = \frac{\mu_0 I_P I_Q}{2\pi r}$$
$$F_l = \frac{\mu_0 I_P I_Q}{2\pi r}$$
特性： 如果兩條導線載有的電流方向相反，它們會相互排斥；如果它們載有的電流方向相同，則會相互吸引。
Characteristics: Two wires repel each other if they carry currents in opposite directions. They attract each other if they carry currents in the same direction.
定義： 每單位長度導線所受的磁力。
Definition: The magnetic force per unit length acting on the wire.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $F_l$
    ：每單位長度的磁力，單位：牛頓每米 ($\text{N m}^{-1}$) : force per unit length, unit: newton per meter ($\text{N m}^{-1}$)
  • $\mu_0$
    ：真空磁導率 ($4\pi \times 10^{-7}\ \text{T m A}^{-1}$) : permeability of free space ($4\pi \times 10^{-7}\ \text{T m A}^{-1}$)
  • $I_P, I_Q$
    ：導線的電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current in the wires, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $r$
    ：導線之間的距離，單位：米 ($\text{m}$) : distance between wires, unit: meter ($\text{m}$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：兩條平行長直導線 X 和 Y 相距 $0.1\ \text{m}$，分別載有 $3\ \text{A}$ 和 $2\ \text{A}$ 的電流。取 $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\ \text{T m A}^{-1}$。求 Y 作用於 X 每單位長度的磁力。 Question: Two long straight parallel wires X and Y are $0.1\ \text{m}$ apart. The currents in X and Y are $3\ \text{A}$ and $2\ \text{A}$ respectively. Take $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\ \text{T m A}^{-1}$. Find the magnetic force per unit length acting on X by Y.
題解：
Solution:
$$F_l = \frac{\mu_0 I_X I_Y}{2\pi r} = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times 3 \times 2}{2\pi \times 0.1} = 1.2 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{N m}^{-1}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：兩條長直平行導線 X 和 Y 相距 $r = 0.02\ \text{m}$。已知導線 X 的電流 $I_X = 3\ \text{A}$，它們之間的每單位長度磁力 $F$ 為 $6 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{N m}^{-1}$，求導線 Y 的電流 $I_Y$。 Question: Two long straight parallel wires X and Y are separated by $r = 0.02\ \text{m}$. Given that the current in X is $I_X = 3\ \text{A}$ and the magnitude of the magnetic force per unit length $F$ is $6 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{N m}^{-1}$, find the current $I_Y in wire Y.
題解：
Solution:
$$I_Y = \frac{F_l \times 2\pi r}{\mu_0 I_X} = \frac{(6 \times 10^{-6}) \times 2\pi \times 0.02}{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times 3} = 0.2\ \text{A}$$

載有相同方向的電流時，互相排斥 Repel each other if carrying currents in the same direction
載有相同方向的電流時，互相吸引 Attract each other if carrying currents in the same direction
無論電流方向為何，總是互相排斥 Always repel each other regardless of current directions
無論電流方向為何，總是互相吸引 Always attract each other regardless of current directions
✅ 正確！如果兩條導線載有相反方向的電流，會相互排斥；如果載有相同方向的電流，則會相互吸引。 ✅ Correct! Two wires repel each other if they carry currents in opposite directions. They attract each other if they carry currents in the same direction.

磁場中的移動電荷 Moving charges in a magnetic field

$$F = BQv \sin \theta$$
圓周運動半徑： 當 $\theta = 90^\circ$ 時，磁力提供向心力，因此 $BQv = \frac{mv^2}{r}$，推導出 $r = \frac{mv}{BQ}$。
Radius of circular motion: When $\theta = 90^\circ$, magnetic force provides centripetal force, $BQv = \frac{mv^2}{r}$, derived to $r = \frac{mv}{BQ}$.
定義： 磁場內的載電流導體會受到磁力作用。其實，這磁力是導體中每個移動電荷所受磁力的總和。
Definition: A magnetic force acts on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field. In fact, this magnetic force is the sum of the force acting on each moving charge in the conductor.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $F$
    ：磁力，單位：牛頓 ($\text{N}$) : magnetic force, unit: newton ($\text{N}$)
  • $B$
    ：磁場，單位：忒斯拉 ($\text{T}$) : magnetic field, unit: tesla ($\text{T}$)
  • $Q$
    ：電荷，單位：庫倫 ($\text{C}$) : charge, unit: coulomb ($\text{C}$)
  • $v$
    ：電荷的速率，單位：米每秒 ($\text{m s}^{-1}$) : speed of charge, unit: meter per second ($\text{m s}^{-1}$)
  • $m$
    ：電荷的質量，單位：千克 ($\text{kg}$) : mass of charge, unit: kilogram ($\text{kg}$)
  • $r$
    ：圓形路徑的半徑，單位：米 ($\text{m}$) : radius of circular path, unit: meter ($\text{m}$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：電荷 $Q = -10\ \mu\text{C}$ 的粒子以 $v = 200\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ 的速率，在 $B = 50\ \text{mT}$ 的勻強磁場中移動。如果移動方向與磁場互相垂直，找出作用於移動電荷的磁力 $F$ 的量值。 Question: A charge $Q = -10\ \mu\text{C}$ moves in a uniform magnetic field $B = 50\ \text{mT}$ at a speed of $v = 200\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. If it moves perpendicular to the magnetic field, find the magnitude of the magnetic force $F$ acting on the moving charge.
題解：
Solution:
$$F = BQv = (50 \times 10^{-3}) \times (10 \times 10^{-6}) \times 200 = 10^{-4}\ \text{N}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：電子以 $1.75 \times 10^6\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ 的速率垂直進入 $0.2\ \text{mT}$ 的勻強磁場。已知電子的質量是 $9.11 \times 10^{-31}\ \text{kg}$，所帶電荷是 $-1.60 \times 10^{-19}\ \text{C}$。電子會在磁場內以圓弧路徑運動。求圓弧的半徑。 Question: An electron enters a $0.2\ \text{mT}$ uniform magnetic field perpendicularly at a speed of $1.75 \times 10^6\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. The mass and charge of an electron are $9.11 \times 10^{-31}\ \text{kg}$ and $-1.60 \times 10^{-19}\ \text{C}$ respectively. The electron travels in a circular arc in the magnetic field. Find the radius of the arc.
題解：
Solution:
$$r = \frac{mv}{BQ} = \frac{(9.11 \times 10^{-31}) \times (1.75 \times 10^6)}{(0.2 \times 10^{-3}) \times (1.60 \times 10^{-19})} = 0.0498\ \text{m}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
帶電粒子 $Q$ 在勻強磁場中移動，若其移動方向垂直於磁場，粒子的路徑是： A charged particle $Q$ moves in a uniform magnetic field. If its moving direction is perpendicular to the magnetic field, what is the path of the particle?
直線 A straight line
拋物線 A parabola
圓形路徑 A circular path
靜止不動 Remains at rest
✅ 正確！磁力提供向心力（$BQv = \frac{mv^2}{r}$），且不對粒子作功，因此電荷會作勻速圓周運動。 ✅ Correct! The magnetic force provides the centripetal force ($BQv = \frac{mv^2}{r}$) and does no work, so the charge performs uniform circular motion.
