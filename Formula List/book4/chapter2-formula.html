電流的定義 Definition of electric current

$$I = \frac{Q}{t}$$
變形公式： $Q = I t$
Derived formula: $Q = I t$
定義： 電流是電荷的流動率。
Definition: An electric current is the rate of flow of electric charges.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $I$
    ：電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : electric current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $Q$
    ：總電荷量，單位：庫倫 ($\text{C}$) : total charge, unit: coulomb ($\text{C}$)
  • $t$
    ：時間，單位：秒 ($\text{s}$) : time, unit: second ($\text{s}$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：電動車以 $80\ \text{A}$ 的電流 $I$ 充電。$2\ \text{minutes}$ 內，共有多少電荷 $Q$ 經過汽車？ Question: An electric car is charged by a current $I$ of $80\ \text{A}$. Find the amount of charge $Q$ flowing through the car in $2\ \text{minutes}$.
題解：
Solution:
$$Q = It = 80 \times (2 \times 60) = 9600\ \text{C}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：電路中，$20\ \text{mA}$ 的電流 $I$ 流過一個電阻器，求 $1\ \text{C}$ 的電荷 $Q$ 流過這電阻器所需的時間 $t$。 Question: A current $I$ of $20\ \text{mA}$ passes through a resistor. Find the time $t$ needed for $1\ \text{C}$ of charge $Q$ to flow through the resistor.
題解：
Solution:
$$t = \frac{Q}{I} = \frac{1}{20 \times 10^{-3}} = 50\ \text{s}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
$1\ \text{A}$ 的電流 $I$ 代表什麼？ What does a current $I$ of $1\ \text{A}$ mean?
每秒有 $1\ \text{J}$ 的能量轉換 $1\ \text{J}$ of energy is converted every second
截面每秒有 $1\ \text{C}$ 的電荷經過 $1\ \text{C}$ of charge passes through the cross-section every second
每秒有 $1$ 個電子經過截面 $1$ electron passes through the cross-section every second
提供 $1\ \text{V}$ 的電勢差 Provides a p.d. of $1\ \text{V}$
✅ 正確！$1\ \text{A}$ 電流代表截面每秒有 $1\ \text{C}$ 的電荷經過。注意公式中 $Q$ 總是取正值。 ✅ Correct! A current of $1\ \text{A}$ means that $1\ \text{C}$ of charge passes through the cross-section every second. Note that $Q$ is always taken as positive.

電動勢、電勢差與電壓 E.m.f., p.d. and voltage

$$V = \frac{E}{Q} \quad \text{或} \quad \varepsilon = \frac{E}{Q}$$
$$V = \frac{E}{Q} \quad \text{or} \quad \varepsilon = \frac{E}{Q}$$
變形公式： $E = VQ \quad \text{與} \quad E = \varepsilon Q$
Derived formulas: $E = VQ \quad \text{and} \quad E = \varepsilon Q$
定義： 電壓 $V$ 是電路中每單位電荷的任何電勢能變化。
Definition: Voltage $V$ is any change in electric PE per unit charge in a circuit.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $V$
    ：電勢差或電壓，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) 或 $\text{J C}^{-1}$ : potential difference or voltage, unit: volt ($\text{V}$) or $\text{J C}^{-1}$
  • $\varepsilon$
    ：電動勢，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) 或 $\text{J C}^{-1}$ : electromotive force (e.m.f.), unit: volt ($\text{V}$) or $\text{J C}^{-1}$
  • $E$
    ：轉換的能量，單位：焦耳 ($\text{J}$) : energy converted, unit: joule ($\text{J}$)
  • $Q$
    ：電荷，單位：庫倫 ($\text{C}$) : charge, unit: coulomb ($\text{C}$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：一顆 $1.2\ \text{V}$ 充電池的容量為 $2950\ \text{mA h}$。充滿電的電池儲存了多少能量 $E$？ Question: A $1.2\text{-V}$ rechargeable battery has a capacity of $2950\ \text{mA h}$. How much energy $E$ is stored in the fully-charged battery?
題解：
1. 先求總電荷量 $Q$：
$$Q = It = (2950 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{A}) \times (3600\ \text{s}) = 1.06 \times 10^4\ \text{C}$$ 2. 儲存的能量 $E$：
$$E = VQ = (1.2\ \text{V}) \times (1.06 \times 10^4\ \text{C}) = 1.27 \times 10^4\ \text{J}$$
Solution:
1. Total charge $Q$:
$$Q = It = (2950 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{A}) \times (3600\ \text{s}) = 1.06 \times 10^4\ \text{C}$$ 2. Stored energy $E$:
$$E = VQ = (1.2\ \text{V}) \times (1.06 \times 10^4\ \text{C}) = 1.27 \times 10^4\ \text{J}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：當 $3\ \text{C}$ 的電荷 $Q$ 通過兩端電勢差 $V$ 為 $4\ \text{V}$ 的燈泡時，負荷所轉換的電勢能 $E$ 是多少？ Question: When $3\ \text{C}$ of charge $Q$ flows through a light bulb with a p.d. $V$ of $4\ \text{V}$ across it, what is the electric PE converted by the load, $E$?
題解：
Solution:
$$E = VQ = 4 \times 3 = 12\ \text{J}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
下列哪一個可以是電動勢 $\varepsilon$ 的單位？ Which of the following can be the unit of e.m.f. $\varepsilon$?
$\text{J}$
$\text{J C}^{-1}$
$\text{J C}$
$\text{A s}$
✅ 正確！電動勢的單位是伏特 ($\text{V}$)，相等於 $\text{J C}^{-1}$。 ✅ Correct! The unit of e.m.f. is the volt ($\text{V}$), which is equal to $\text{J C}^{-1}$.

電阻與電阻率 Resistance and resistivity

$$R = \frac{V}{I} \quad \text{與} \quad R = \rho \frac{l}{A}$$
$$R = \frac{V}{I} \quad \text{and} \quad R = \rho \frac{l}{A}$$
變形公式： $V = I R \quad \text{與} \quad \rho = \frac{R A}{l}$
Derived formulas: $V = I R \quad \text{and} \quad \rho = \frac{R A}{l}$
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $R$
    ：電阻，單位：歐姆 ($\Omega$) : resistance, unit: ohm ($\Omega$)
  • $V$
    ：電壓，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) : voltage, unit: volt ($\text{V}$)
  • $I$
    ：電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $\rho$
    ：電阻率，單位：歐姆·米 ($\Omega\text{ m}$) : resistivity, unit: ohm-meter ($\Omega\text{ m}$)
  • $l$
    ：導線長度，單位：米 ($\text{m}$) : length, unit: meter ($\text{m}$)
  • $A$
    ：截面積，單位：平方米 ($\text{m}^2$) : cross-sectional area, unit: square meter ($\text{m}^2$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：燈泡連接到 $3\ \text{V}$ 電池組。流過燈泡的電流 $I$ 是 $0.3\ \text{A}$。求燈泡的電阻 $R$。 Question: A light bulb is connected to a $3\text{-V}$ battery. The current $I$ through the bulb is $0.3\ \text{A}$. Find the resistance $R$ of the bulb.
題解：
Solution:
$$R = \frac{V}{I} = \frac{3}{0.3} = 10\ \Omega$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：銅線長度 $l = 10\ \text{m}$，截面積 $A = 1.3 \times 10^{-7}\ \text{m}^2$，電阻 $R = 1.34\ \Omega$。求銅線的電阻率 $\rho$。 Question: A copper wire is $10\ \text{m}$ long ($l$). Its cross-sectional area $A$ is $1.3 \times 10^{-7}\ \text{m}^2$. Its resistance $R$ is $1.34\ \Omega$. Find the resistivity $\rho$ of the copper wire.
題解：
Solution:
$$\rho = \frac{R A}{l} = \frac{1.34 \times (1.3 \times 10^{-7})}{10} = 1.74 \times 10^{-8}\ \Omega\text{ m}$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
對於相同物料的導線，以下哪種情況會有較低的電阻 $R$？ For wires of the same material, which of the following gives a lower resistance $R$?
電阻線長度 $l$ 愈長 A longer length $l$ wire
電阻線愈粗（截面積 $A$ 較大） A thicker wire (larger cross-sectional area $A$)
電阻線愈幼（截面積 $A$ 較小） A thinner wire (smaller cross-sectional area $A$)
長度 $l$ 與截面積 $A$ 不影響電阻 Length $l$ and area $A$ do not affect resistance
✅ 正確！電阻 $R$ 與截面積 $A$ 成反比，電阻線愈粗（截面積 $A$ 愈大），電阻 $R$ 愈低。 ✅ Correct! Resistance $R$ is inversely proportional to the cross-sectional area $A$, so a thicker wire has a lower resistance.

電阻器網絡 Resistor networks

$$\text{串聯：} R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 + ... + R_n$$ $$\text{並聯：} \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + ... + \frac{1}{R_n}$$
$$\text{In series: } R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 + ... + R_n$$ $$\text{In parallel: } \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + ... + \frac{1}{R_n}$$
電路特性與變形公式：
串聯電路中，總電壓 $V = V_1 + V_2$，通過每一點的電流相同 $I = I_1 = I_2$。
並聯電路中，橫跨每個支路的電勢差相同 $V = V_1 = V_2$，總電流 $I = I_1 + I_2$。
Circuit characteristics and derived formulas:
In a series circuit, total voltage $V = V_1 + V_2$, current is the same at all points $I = I_1 = I_2$.
In a parallel circuit, p.d. across each branch is the same $V = V_1 = V_2$, total current $I = I_1 + I_2$.
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $R_{eq}$
    ：等效電阻，單位：歐姆 ($\Omega$) : equivalent resistance, unit: ohm ($\Omega$)
  • $R_1, R_2, R_n$
    ：個別電阻器的電阻，單位：歐姆 ($\Omega$) : resistance of individual resistors, unit: ohm ($\Omega$)
  • $V$
    ：總電壓，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) : total voltage, unit: volt ($\text{V}$)
  • $V_1, V_2$
    ：個別電阻器兩端的電勢差，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) : p.d. across individual resistors, unit: volt ($\text{V}$)
  • $I$
    ：總電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : total current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $I_1, I_2$
    ：通過個別電阻器的電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current through individual resistors, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：兩個電阻器（$R_1 = 2\ \Omega$ 與 $R_2 = 4\ \Omega$）串聯連接到 $12\ \text{V}$ 電池組。求等效電阻 $R_{eq}$。 Question: Two resistors ($R_1 = 2\ \Omega$ and $R_2 = 4\ \Omega$) are connected in series to a $12\text{-V}$ battery. What is the equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$?
題解：
Solution:
$$R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 = 2 + 4 = 6\ \Omega$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：兩個電阻器並聯連接到電池組。電池組輸出的總電流 $I$ 是 $0.5\ \text{A}$，電池組電動勢 $\varepsilon$ 為 $6\ \text{V}$。求等效電阻 $R_{eq}$。 Question: Two resistors are connected in parallel to a battery. The total current $I$ drawn from the battery is $0.5\ \text{A}$, and the e.m.f. $\varepsilon$ is $6\ \text{V}$. What is the equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$?
題解：
Solution:
$$R_{eq} = \frac{V}{I} = \frac{6}{0.5} = 12\ \Omega$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
關於並聯的電阻器，下列哪項敘述必定正確？ Which of the following statements about resistors connected in parallel must be correct?
等效電阻 $R_{eq}$ 高於個別電阻器 $R_n$ 的電阻 The equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ is larger than any single resistor $R_n$
橫跨每個電阻器的電勢差 $V_n$ 都相同 The p.d. $V_n$ across each resistor is the same
通過每個電阻器的電流 $I_n$ 都相同 The current $I_n$ through each resistor is the same
等效電阻 $R_{eq}$ 等於各電阻之和 The equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ is the sum of the resistors
✅ 正確！在並聯電路中，橫跨每個電阻器的電勢差都相同 ($V = V_1 = V_2$)。 ✅ Correct! In a parallel circuit, the p.d. across each resistor is the same ($V = V_1 = V_2$).

電功率 Electrical power

$$P = VI$$
結合歐姆定律的變形公式：
Derived formulas based on Ohm's law:
$$P = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$
📐 變數與單位解釋 (含變形公式) 📐 Variables and Units (Including derived equations)
  • $P$
    ：電功率，單位：瓦特 ($\text{W}$) : electrical power, unit: watt ($\text{W}$)
  • $V$
    ：電壓，單位：伏特 ($\text{V}$) : voltage, unit: volt ($\text{V}$)
  • $I$
    ：電流，單位：安培 ($\text{A}$) : current, unit: ampere ($\text{A}$)
  • $R$
    ：電阻，單位：歐姆 ($\Omega$) : resistance, unit: ohm ($\Omega$)
✏️ 應用範例 ✏️ Example
題目：燈泡 A 和 B 的電阻分別為 $R_A = 1\ \Omega$ 和 $R_B = 2\ \Omega$。如果把它們並聯連接到 $3\ \text{V}$ 電池組，求燈泡 A 消耗的功率 $P_A$。 Question: Light bulbs A and B have resistances of $R_A = 1\ \Omega$ and $R_B = 2\ \Omega$ respectively. If they are connected in parallel to a $3\text{-V}$ battery, find the power $P_A$ consumed by bulb A.
題解：
Solution:
$$P_A = \frac{V^2}{R_A} = \frac{3^2}{1} = 9\ \text{W}$$
📝 隨堂練習 📝 Practice
題目：燈泡連接到 $V = 1.5\ \text{V}$ 電池組時，消耗的功率 $P$ 為 $0.45\ \text{W}$。求燈泡的電阻 $R$。 Question: A light bulb consumes a power $P$ of $0.45\ \text{W}$ when connected to a $V = 1.5\text{-V}$ battery. Find its resistance $R$.
題解：根據 $P = \frac{V^2}{R}$ 變形，
Solution: From $P = \frac{V^2}{R}$,
$$R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{1.5^2}{0.45} = 5\ \Omega$$
🧠 概念測驗 🧠 Concept Quiz
一個 $2\ \Omega$ 電阻器和一個 $6\ \Omega$ 電阻器串聯連接到電池組。哪一個消耗的功率 $P$ 較高？ A $2\text{-}\Omega$ resistor and a $6\text{-}\Omega$ resistor are connected in series to a battery. Which one dissipates a higher power $P$?
$2\ \Omega$ 電阻器$2\ \Omega$ resistor
$6\ \Omega$ 電阻器$6\ \Omega$ resistor
兩者消耗的功率 $P$ 相同Both dissipate the same power $P$
無法確定Cannot be determined
✅ 正確！串聯時電流 $I$ 相同，根據 $P = I^2 R$，電阻 $R$ 較高的一個會消耗較高的功率 $P$。 ✅ Correct! In series, the current $I$ is the same. By $P = I^2 R$, the one with a higher resistance $R$ dissipates a higher power $P$.
