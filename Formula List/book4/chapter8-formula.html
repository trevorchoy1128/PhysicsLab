簡單變壓器 Simple Transformer

$$\frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}$$
$$V_s = \frac{N_s}{N_p} \times V_p \quad , \quad V_p = \frac{N_p}{N_s} \times V_s$$
符號意義 Symbol List
  • $V_p$
    ：原電壓，單位：$\text{V}$ : primary voltage, unit: $\text{V}$
  • $V_s$
    ：副電壓，單位：$\text{V}$ : secondary voltage, unit: $\text{V}$
  • $N_p$
    ：原線圈匝數 : number of turns in primary coil
  • $N_s$
    ：副線圈匝數 : number of turns in secondary coil
💡 例題 💡 Example
變壓器的原線圈連接到 $220\ \text{V}$ 市電。原線圈有 $2200$ 匝，副線圈有 $110$ 匝。副線圈兩端的電壓是多少？ The primary coil of a transformer is connected to the $220\ \text{V}$ mains. There are $2200$ turns in the primary coil and $110$ turns in the secondary coil. What is the voltage across the secondary coil?
根據 $\frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}$，
$V_s = \frac{N_s}{N_p} \times V_p = \frac{110}{2200} \times 220 = 11\ \text{V}$。
By $\frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}$,
$V_s = \frac{N_s}{N_p} \times V_p = \frac{110}{2200} \times 220 = 11\ \text{V}$.
✏️ 隨堂練習 ✏️ Practice
變壓器的原線圈連接至 $20\ \text{V}$ 交流電源。副線圈兩端的電壓為 $100\ \text{V}$。求變壓器的匝數比。 The primary coil of a transformer is connected to a $20\ \text{V}$ a.c. supply. The voltage across the secondary coil is $100\ \text{V}$. Find the turns ratio of the transformer.
$\frac{N_p}{N_s} = \frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{20}{100} = \frac{1}{5}$。
變壓器的匝數比是 $1:5$。
$\frac{N_p}{N_s} = \frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{20}{100} = \frac{1}{5}$.
The turns ratio is $1:5$.
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz
下列哪項有關變壓器的敍述正確？ Which of the following statements about a transformer is correct?
穩定的直流電通過原線圈時，沒有磁場產生。 No magnetic field is produced if a steady d.c. passes through the primary coil.
只要通過副線圈的磁通量有所變化，副線圈就會感生出電動勢。 An e.m.f. is induced in the secondary coil as long as the secondary coil experiences a change in magnetic flux.
變壓器只能以交流電操作。 The transformer only works with a.c.
副電壓必定低於原電壓。 The secondary voltage is always lower than the primary voltage.
答對了！根據法拉第電磁感應定律，只要磁通量有變化即可感生電動勢。 Correct! According to Faraday's law, an e.m.f. is induced as long as there is a change in magnetic flux.
答錯了。請注意穩定的直流電仍會產生磁場（只是磁場不變），變壓器也可用不斷變化的直流電操作，且升壓器的副電壓大於原電壓。 Incorrect. Note that steady d.c. still produces a magnetic field (just not varying), transformers can work with varying d.c., and step-up transformers have a higher secondary voltage.

實用變壓器的效率 Efficiency of Practical Transformer

$$\text{效率} = \frac{P_s}{P_p} \times 100\% = \frac{V_s I_s}{V_p I_p} \times 100\%$$
$$\text{Efficiency} = \frac{P_s}{P_p} \times 100\% = \frac{V_s I_s}{V_p I_p} \times 100\%$$
理想變壓器（效率 $= 100\%$）：
Ideal transformer (Efficiency $= 100\%$):
$$V_p I_p = V_s I_s \implies \frac{I_s}{I_p} = \frac{V_p}{V_s} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}$$
符號意義 Symbol List
  • $P_p$
    ：原線圈的功率輸入，單位：$\text{W}$ : power input to primary coil, unit: $\text{W}$
  • $P_s$
    ：副線圈的功率輸出，單位：$\text{W}$ : power output from secondary coil, unit: $\text{W}$
  • $I_p$
    ：原電流，單位：$\text{A}$ : primary current, unit: $\text{A}$
  • $I_s$
    ：副電流，單位：$\text{A}$ : secondary current, unit: $\text{A}$
💡 例題 💡 Example
燈泡經變壓器連接至 $220\ \text{V}$ 市電。變壓器的匝數比是 $11:1$。燈泡消耗的功率是 $50\ \text{W}$。原電流是 $0.3\ \text{A}$。求變壓器的效率。 A light bulb is connected to the $220\ \text{V}$ mains via a transformer. The turns ratio of the transformer is $11:1$. The power consumed by the bulb is $50\ \text{W}$. The primary current is $0.3\ \text{A}$. Find the efficiency of the transformer.
$P_p = V_p I_p = (220)(0.3) = 66\ \text{W}$。
效率 $= \frac{P_s}{P_p} \times 100\% = \frac{50}{66} \times 100\% = 75.8\%$。
$P_p = V_p I_p = (220)(0.3) = 66\ \text{W}$.
Efficiency $= \frac{P_s}{P_p} \times 100\% = \frac{50}{66} \times 100\% = 75.8\%$.
✏️ 隨堂練習 ✏️ Practice
負荷連接到變壓器，原電壓是 $240\ \text{V}$，原電流是 $2\ \text{A}$，副電壓是 $100\ \text{V}$，副電流是 $4\ \text{A}$。求變壓器的效率。 A load is connected to a transformer. The primary voltage is $240\ \text{V}$. The primary current is $2\ \text{A}$. The secondary voltage is $100\ \text{V}$. The secondary current is $4\ \text{A}$. Find the efficiency of the transformer.
效率 $= \frac{V_s I_s}{V_p I_p} \times 100\% = \frac{100 \times 4}{240 \times 2} \times 100\% = 83.3\%$。
Efficiency $= \frac{V_s I_s}{V_p I_p} \times 100\% = \frac{100 \times 4}{240 \times 2} \times 100\% = 83.3\%$.
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz
減少變壓器功率損耗的方法是甚麼？ How can the power loss of a transformer be reduced?
使用較幼的導線來繞成線圈 Using thinner wires to make the coil
使用鋼來製造磁心 Using steel to make the core
使用實心鐵心 Using a solid core
使用疊片式鐵心 Using a laminated core
答對了！使用疊片式鐵心可以減少渦電流損耗。 Correct! Using a laminated core reduces eddy current loss.
答錯了。較幼的導線會增加電阻；鋼不易磁化和去磁；實心鐵心會產生較大的渦電流。正確方法是使用疊片式鐵心。 Incorrect. Thinner wires increase resistance; steel is hard to magnetize/demagnetize; a solid core produces large eddy currents. The correct method is using a laminated core.

輸電 Transmission of Electricity

$$\text{功率損耗} = I^2 R$$
$$\text{Power loss} = I^2 R$$
$$I = \frac{P}{V_T} \implies \text{功率損耗} = \left(\frac{P}{V_T}\right)^2 R$$
$$I = \frac{P}{V_T} \implies \text{Power loss} = \left(\frac{P}{V_T}\right)^2 R$$
符號意義 Symbol List
  • $I$
    ：通過電纜的電流，單位：$\text{A}$ : current through the cables, unit: $\text{A}$
  • $R$
    ：電纜的電阻，單位：$\Omega$ : resistance of the cables, unit: $\Omega$
  • $P$
    ：傳送的電功率，單位：$\text{W}$ : electrical power transmitted, unit: $\text{W}$
  • $V_T$
    ：輸電電壓，單位：$\text{V}$ : transmitting voltage, unit: $\text{V}$
💡 例題 💡 Example
一段總電阻為 $5\ \Omega$ 的電纜以電壓 $2\ \text{kV}$ 把 $4\ \text{kW}$ 的電功率傳送到負荷。找出輸電過程中功率損耗的百分比。 Electrical power of $4\ \text{kW}$ is transmitted from a power supply to a load at $2\ \text{kV}$ via transmission cables with a total resistance of $5\ \Omega$. Find the percentage of power loss during transmission.
通過電纜的電流 $= \frac{P}{V_T} = \frac{4000}{2000} = 2\ \text{A}$。
電纜的功率損耗 $= I^2 R = (2)^2(5) = 20\ \text{W}$。
功率損耗的百分比 $= \frac{20}{4000} \times 100\% = 0.5\%$。
Current through cables $= \frac{P}{V_T} = \frac{4000}{2000} = 2\ \text{A}$.
Power loss in cables $= I^2 R = (2)^2(5) = 20\ \text{W}$.
Percentage of power loss $= \frac{20}{4000} \times 100\% = 0.5\%$.
✏️ 隨堂練習 ✏️ Practice
總電阻為 $2\ \Omega$ 的電纜輸送 $20\ \text{MW}$ 電力。假設電力以 $20\ \text{kV}$ 傳輸。求電纜的功率損耗。 Electrical power of $20\ \text{MW}$ is transmitted via transmission cables with a total resistance of $2\ \Omega$. Suppose the power is transmitted at $20\ \text{kV}$. Find the power loss in the cables.
$I = \frac{P}{V_T} = \frac{20 \times 10^6}{20 \times 10^3} = 1000\ \text{A}$。
功率損耗 $= I^2 R = (1000)^2 \times 2 = 2 \times 10^6\ \text{W} = 2\ \text{MW}$。
$I = \frac{P}{V_T} = \frac{20 \times 10^6}{20 \times 10^3} = 1000\ \text{A}$.
Power loss $= I^2 R = (1000)^2 \times 2 = 2 \times 10^6\ \text{W} = 2\ \text{MW}$.
🎯 概念測驗 🎯 Quiz
為甚麼輸電時使用高電壓？ Why is high voltage used in power transmission?
可降低電纜的電阻 This reduces the resistance of the transmission cables.
可減少通過電纜的電流 This reduces the current through the transmission cables.
交流電可以輕易地升壓或降壓 A.c. can be stepped up or down easily.
交流電通過電纜時沒有功率損耗 No power is lost when a.c. flows through the transmission cables.
答對了！根據 $P = V_T I$，當傳送功率相同時，提高電壓能減少電流，進而大幅降低 $I^2 R$ 的功率損耗。 Correct! By $P = V_T I$, for the same power transmitted, higher voltage reduces the current, thus significantly reducing $I^2 R$ power loss.
答錯了。電纜電阻取決於長度和材質；容易升降壓是交流電的好處，但不是高壓輸電直接減少損耗的根本原因。真正原因是減少了電流。 Incorrect. Cable resistance depends on material and length; ease of voltage change is an advantage of a.c., but not the root cause of reduced power loss. The true reason is the reduction of current.
