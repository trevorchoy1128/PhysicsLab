🧵 長直導線 🧵 Long straight wires

$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$
$$I = \frac{2\pi r B}{\mu_0} \quad \text{,} \quad r = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi B}$$
📊 變數與單位解釋 📊 Symbol List
  • $B$磁場 ($\text{T}$)Magnetic field ($\text{T}$)
  • $\mu_0$真空磁導率 ($4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$)Permeability of free space ($4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$)
  • $I$電流 ($\text{A}$)Current ($\text{A}$)
  • $r$與導線的距離 ($\text{m}$)Distance from wire ($\text{m}$)
💡 範例💡 Example

$0.5 \text{ A}$ 的電流通過一條長直導線。$P$ 點與導線的距離是 $d$，在 $P$ 點磁場的強度是 $4 \mu\text{T}$。求 $d$。取 $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$。 A current of $0.5 \text{ A}$ passes through a long straight wire. Point $P$ is at a distance $d$ from the wire and the magnetic field there has a strength of $4 \mu\text{T}$. Find $d$. Take $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$.

🔑 題解🔑 Solution
根據 $$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

$$4 \times 10^{-6} = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times (0.5)}{2\pi(d)}$$

$$d = 0.025 \text{ m}$$
By $$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

$$4 \times 10^{-6} = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times (0.5)}{2\pi(d)}$$

$$d = 0.025 \text{ m}$$
📝 隨堂練習📝 Practice

兩條平行長直導線 $X$ 和 $Y$ 分別載有 $2 \text{ A}$ 和 $3 \text{ A}$ 電流，方向指入頁面。兩者相距 $6 \text{ cm}$。$Q$ 點是導線 $X$ 和 $Y$ 之間的一個中和點。求 $Q$ 點與導線 $Y$ 的距離 $d$。 Two parallel long wires $X$ and $Y$ carry currents of $2 \text{ A}$ and $3 \text{ A}$ respectively into the page. They are $6 \text{ cm}$ apart. $Q$ is a neutral point between wires $X$ and $Y$. Find the distance $d$ of $Q$ from wire $Y$.

🔑 題解🔑 Solution
在 $Q$ 點，導線 $X$ 產生的磁場 ($B_X$) 指向下，而導線 $Y$ 產生的磁場 ($B_Y$) 指向上。它們互相抵消。

因此，根據 $$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$，

$$B_X = B_Y$$

$$\frac{\mu_0(2)}{2\pi(0.06 - d)} = \frac{\mu_0(3)}{2\pi(d)}$$

$$2d = 3(0.06 - d)$$

$$d = 0.036 \text{ m}$$
At $Q$, the magnetic field due to $X$ ($B_X$) points downwards and the magnetic field due to $Y$ ($B_Y$) points upwards. They cancel each other out.

Therefore, by $$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$,

$$B_X = B_Y$$

$$\frac{\mu_0(2)}{2\pi(0.06 - d)} = \frac{\mu_0(3)}{2\pi(d)}$$

$$2d = 3(0.06 - d)$$,

$$d = 0.036 \text{ m}$$

🌀 長螺線管內 🌀 Inside a long solenoid

$$B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{l} = \mu_0 n I$$
$$n = \frac{N}{l} \quad \text{,} \quad I = \frac{B l}{\mu_0 N}$$
📊 變數與單位解釋 📊 Symbol List
  • $B$磁場 ($\text{T}$)Magnetic field ($\text{T}$)
  • $\mu_0$真空磁導率 ($4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$)Permeability of free space ($4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$)
  • $N$匝數 (沒有單位)Number of turns (no unit)
  • $l$長度 ($\text{m}$)Length ($\text{m}$)
  • $I$電流 ($\text{A}$)Current ($\text{A}$)
  • $n$每單位長度的匝數 ($\text{m}^{-1}$)Number of turns per unit length ($\text{m}^{-1}$)
💡 範例💡 Example

某個長螺線管有 $200$ 匝，長 $25 \text{ cm}$，有 $2 \text{ A}$ 的電流通過。取 $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$。在螺線管內接近中心點處，磁場的強度是多少？ A current of $2 \text{ A}$ is flowing through a long solenoid having a length of $25 \text{ cm}$ and $200$ turns. Take $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T m A}^{-1}$. Find the strength of the magnetic field near the centre inside the solenoid.

🔑 題解🔑 Solution
$$B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{l}$$

$$B = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times 200 \times 2}{0.25}$$

$$B = 2.01 \times 10^{-3} \text{ T}$$

📝 隨堂練習📝 Practice

某螺線管有 $N$ 匝，長度是 $L$。電流 $I$ 通過它時，它內部的磁場是 $B_0$。如果螺線管被拉長到 $2L$，而通過它的電流變成 $2I$，那麼螺線管內的磁場是多少？ A solenoid has $N$ turns and its length is $L$. When a current $I$ passes through it, the magnetic field inside it is $B_0$. What is the magnetic field inside it if it is stretched to a length $2L$ and the current passing through it becomes $2I$?

🔑 題解🔑 Solution
原先的磁場 $$B_0 = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{L}$$。

新的磁場 $$B' = \frac{\mu_0 N (2I)}{2L} = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{L} = B_0$$。

因此，磁場保持不變。
The original magnetic field is $$B_0 = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{L}$$.

The new magnetic field is $$B' = \frac{\mu_0 N (2I)}{2L} = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{L} = B_0$$.

Therefore, the magnetic field remains unchanged.

在以下哪些情況中，螺線管中心的磁場會增加至原來的兩倍？ In which of the following situations will the magnetic field at the centre of a solenoid be doubled?

電流增加至原來的兩倍。 The current is doubled.
把兩個相同的螺線管串聯，使螺線管的長度增加至原來的兩倍。 The length of the solenoid is doubled by connecting an identical solenoid in series.
線圈的間距增加至原來的兩倍。 The separation of the turns is doubled.
螺線管的直徑增加至原來的兩倍。 The diameter of the solenoid is doubled.
根據公式 $$B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{l} = \mu_0 n I$$，磁場與電流 $I$ 及每單位長度的匝數 $n$成正比。因此電流加倍時，磁場也會加倍。其他選項中，串聯會保持 $n$ 不變；直徑不影響內部磁場；增加間距會減小 $n$。 According to $$B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{l} = \mu_0 n I$$, the magnetic field is directly proportional to current $I$ and number of turns per unit length $n$. So doubling the current doubles the field. Connecting in series keeps $n$ constant; diameter doesn't affect it; doubling separation halves $n$.
